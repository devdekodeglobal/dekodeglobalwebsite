The DEKODE STAR Standard

Four promises behind everything we deliver. STAR is how we work and how you can hold us to account:

Simple

What it means: plain language, focused scope, priorities tied to business value. Your guarantee: proposals, updates, and products you can understand without a technical background.

Transparent

What it means: you always know what is being built, what it costs, and how success is measured. Your guarantee: visible progress, honest estimates, no surprise costs , changes agreed in writing first.

Accountable

What it means: we own the outcome, not just the output. Your guarantee: if something is not working, we own it, diagnose it, and fix it properly , named owner, clear next step.

Reliable

What it means: security and stability built in from day one. Your guarantee: a tested, stable product with ongoing support options.

The STAR test: every milestone should feel Simple to understand, Transparent in cost and progress, Accountable in ownership, and Reliable in quality. If any of the four slips, raise it , we will correct it.

Design Standards: Clear & Simple

Your users should understand the product instantly, with minimal effort. Here is what you will see and feel:

Clean, readable typography

Clear sans-serif fonts (such as Inter), generous sizes , never tiny unreadable text , and strong contrast that stays crisp on any screen. Benefit: less eye strain, fewer reading errors.

Minimalist screens, progressive detail

Summary numbers first, common filters second, technical detail tucked behind expandable panels. Benefit: new users feel confident immediately.

Accessible contrast, light and dark comfort

Text contrast meeting recognised accessibility guidance, comfortable light and dark viewing modes, calm gold-accented visuals. Benefit: usable in bright offices, dim warehouses, and for users with visual difficulties.

Responsive on phone, tablet, desktop

Layouts adapt , tables scroll gracefully, cards stack into single columns, nothing important is cut off. Benefit: approve, check, and act from a phone on the move.

Generous spacing, concise labels

Breathing room around content and short, plain labels instead of clutter. Benefit: complex information becomes glanceable , fewer training hours.

Consistent, calm interactions

Buttons, forms, and confirmations behave the same way everywhere. Benefit: fewer user mistakes, fewer support tickets.

What to judge in a demo: Can a first-time user read everything without squinting? Is the most important number obvious within five seconds? Does it work on a phone? If yes, our design standards are doing their job.

Engagement Process

1. Discover→2. Design→3. Build→4. Validate→5. Launch & Support
  1. Discover , goals, users, scope, success measures agreed together.
  2. Design , solution outline and plan for your approval before build.
  3. Build , iterative delivery with demos at each checkpoint.
  4. Validate , thorough testing, fixes, and your formal acceptance sign-off.
  5. Launch and support , release, handover summary, and agreed aftercare terms.
Nothing moves to the next stage without your sign-off. That keeps surprises out and control with you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions clients ask most:

How does a project typically start?

With a short discovery conversation to agree goals, users, scope, and how success will be measured. You receive a written proposal with scope, phased plan, and checkpoints , work begins only after your sign-off.

How long will my project take?

It depends on scope, but phasing keeps it predictable: each phase has its own agreed timeline and demo. Because you see working versions early, risks surface while they are still cheap to fix. You receive a phased estimate before approving the full build.

What will I actually receive and when?

An agreed plan, working product versions at each checkpoint, a tested release with plain-language notes, and a handover summary with support terms. Demos precede every sign-off, so what you approve is what gets built.

How do you ensure quality and testing?

Quality is built in at every stage. Work is checked against your acceptance criteria with layered testing , from individual features up to the full product , plus independent review before release. Anything that fails is fixed before sign-off is requested.

How do you handle security and our confidential information?

Security is part of the build from day one. Your confidential information and access credentials are handled with strict care , never exposed in working materials , and access is limited to those who need it. Releases are reviewed for safe data handling before launch.

Who owns the work and our data?

You do. On handover, ownership of the agreed deliverables transfers to you as contracted, with a summary your team can use. Your data remains yours at all times, and historical records are preserved so decisions stay traceable.

How do we track progress and communicate?

Through one accountable project lead, regular status updates, and checkpoint demos. You always know what is done, what is next, what is needed from you, and who owns each open item.

What happens when we need to change scope?

Tell us as early as possible. We assess the impact on time and cost, confirm it with you in writing, and proceed only after your approval. Phased delivery means most changes fit into an upcoming phase without derailing what is agreed.

How do you choose technology for our project?

We recommend proven, well-supported technologies matched to your needs , performance, lifespan, team skills, and budget , and explain the trade-offs in plain language. The goal is a product dependable today and maintainable for years.

What support do we get after launch?

Launch includes handover plus agreed aftercare. Ongoing support and improvement options are confirmed at handover , with a clear owner, response expectations, and a path for fixes and enhancements.

What do you need from us to succeed?

Timely decisions, access to the right stakeholders and information, and prompt feedback at checkpoints. Projects move fastest when sign-offs arrive on schedule , we always make clear what is needed and by when.
