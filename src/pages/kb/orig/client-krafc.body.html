krafc
Draw your floor plan. Walk through it in 3D , instantly, in your browser.

krafc is a web-based space-planning platform for exhibition stands, interiors and retail fit-outs. Sketch a 2D floor plan and immediately see a realistic 3D view. No installs, no CAD training, one click to a professional quote-ready report.

Live 2D → 3D Synchronization

Draw walls and place furniture on an intuitive 2D canvas; walk through the photorealistic 3D environment in real time.

Browser-Native & Zero CAD Setup

Zero software installations or expensive licenses. Accessible on any desktop or tablet with instant quote-ready report generation.

Why krafc exists: getting just 3 stand options used to mean a designer spending weeks on hand-built 3D models, with every revision billed extra and sign-off dragging out for nearly a month. krafc compresses that into live working sessions. Our showcase projects tell the story: a 21 sqm tech booth, a 70 sqm lounge and bar, and a 47.5 sqm enterprise pavilion, each going from bare dimensions to a live 3D walkthrough in about five minutes.
01

What is krafc?

krafc replaces the slow back-and-forth of sketches, renders and spreadsheets with one live workspace. Your designer draws the layout in 2D; you see the finished stand in 3D at the same time. It is built for speed, clarity and confident sign-off before anything is built.

Zero-Install Fluidity

Runs entirely in any modern browser on desktop or tablet. Nothing to install, no heavy CAD licences.

Real-Time Split View

Move a wall or sofa on the plan and the 3D view updates instantly with lighting and shadows.

Quote-Ready Reports

One click produces visuals, layouts and item lists your team can approve and procure from.

Design philosophy: “What You Draw Is What You Walk Through”
The 2D plan is the source of truth , the 3D view always matches it.

Project Flow: From Kickoff to Delivery

1. Set Space

Client + Designer

Exact booth boundaries in metres, wall topology: corner, U-shape, enclosed or island.

→

2. Blueprint + Layout

Designer Live Session

Precision 2D grid snapping. Fixtures, furniture, storage zones and partitions placed live.

→

3. 3D Walkthrough

Client + Designer

Real-time orbit and first-person clearance checks with lighting and textures, in the same session.

→

4. Dossier + Approval

Contractor + Client

Renders, dimensioned plans and bill of materials in one quote-ready PDF for sign-off and build.

The approved dossier goes straight to production and on-site build, no re-draws. Layouts can be saved, duplicated and adapted for your next show.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install anything or learn CAD?
No. krafc runs in your browser. If you can drag and click, you can review and comment on your design.
How fast can I see my stand in 3D?
Instantly. Every change on the 2D plan appears in the 3D view in real time , no waiting for renders.
Can I share feedback with my team?
Yes. The approval dossier includes clear visuals and item lists that are easy to circulate for sign-off.
Does it work on tablet?
Yes, krafc works on modern desktop and tablet browsers.
Who owns my project designs?
You do. Your project spaces and exported dossiers are yours to keep, reuse and adapt.
Why was krafc built?
Because the old way hurt: getting just 3 design options meant a designer spending weeks hand-building 3D models, every revision cost extra, and decisions stalled waiting for re-renders. krafc was built so you can explore unlimited options live and approve with confidence, in days, not weeks.
How much does it save compared to commissioning 3 models?
Traditional visualisation charges per model and per revision round. With krafc there are no per-model fees, you walk through options in real time and revise as often as you like during design, so the cost of the second, third, or tenth option is effectively zero.
Can we compare multiple design options side by side?
Yes. Layout variants that used to take a designer days each can now be created and walked through in minutes, so stakeholders compare real options instead of imagining them from sketches.
How accurate is the 3D view versus the final built stand?
The 2D plan is the source of truth and everything is drawn to your real measurements, so what you walk through is what gets built. The quote-ready dossier carries the same dimensions, visuals, and item lists into procurement.
What do you need from us to start a project?
Floor dimensions, your brand assets and product/item list, and any must-haves for the space. From that we set up your project and you are reviewing live 3D in the first session.
What happens when we request changes mid-project?
Changes happen live in the review session, move a wall, swap furniture, and everyone sees the updated 3D instantly. No re-render queues, no extra visualisation invoices, no waiting a week to see a moved sofa.
Can non-designers and senior stakeholders participate?
That is the point. Anyone who can drag and click can explore the space and leave feedback. Sign-off becomes a shared walkthrough instead of a designer presenting static renders.
Do you provide training and support?
Yes. A short onboarding session gets your team comfortable, and we support you through your first live project including the approval dossier and handover.
Is the 3D accurate enough to actually build from?
Yes. Everything is drawn to your real measurements with millimetre snapping, and the dossier carries dimensions, clearances and item lists that contractors build from directly.
Do we need powerful computers or software licences?
No. krafc runs entirely in a standard web browser. Nothing to install, no heavy hardware, no CAD licences.
